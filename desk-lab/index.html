Desk shape lab

Drag desks together and watch them mate. The app's Unique shape has two exactly congruent curved edges (chord 115.004, bulge 40.002 on both), so all nine of its mates lock flush. The real MooreCo Hierarchy Shapes outline, traced from page 3 of the manufacturer's spec drawing, has no congruent pair at all — its closest match is off by 4.7% — so every arrangement either overlaps or gapes. Joint readings show how much edge is actually touching, not the nearest point, because two desks can kiss at a corner while the mated edges hang wide open.
